This is the beta version of the 162-0 baseball game.  
This looks at hitting only in this version.
Spin the wheels per draft pick, it will give you a Team and a Decade.  The top 15 hitters will be listed with their best season stats.  
Select 1 player at a time and assign to a position.  There is one DH field that any position can be added to.  
Once your lineup is completed, the game will calculate your expected record.  
Can you go 162-0?
